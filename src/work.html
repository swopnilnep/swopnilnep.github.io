Principles

A few ideas I come back to when designing systems and working with teams:

  • First principles: Start from what must be true, not from how it's usually done. Break a problem down to its fundamentals before choosing a solution.
  • Second-order thinking: Ask what happens next. A change that saves time today can add cost, load, or risk somewhere else later.
  • Systems thinking: Look at feedback loops and at how a change travels through connected parts. Inspired by Thinking in Systems.
  • Conway's law: Systems mirror the teams that build them, so service boundaries and team boundaries should be designed together.
  • Jevons paradox: When something gets cheaper, we use more of it. AI makes code cheap to write, so clear requirements, review, testing, and agreement on what a system is for matter more, not less.
